Battery costs

Prices in pence per kWh, as on your bill. For a single-rate tariff, use the same price for peak and off-peak.

Prices come from your Octopus account, so only the battery's price is used from here.

Octopus is connected but its prices haven't loaded yet, so these typed prices are used for now. See Electricity prices for why.

With the install date, savings from before this dashboard was running are estimated from the battery's lifetime totals.

Octopus Energy

Reads your tariff and prices so you don't have to type them in. On octopus.energy, open Account > Personal details > API access for your key. Nothing is changed on your account.

Set by OCTOPUS_API_KEY and OCTOPUS_ACCOUNT in the container settings. Remove them from your stack to change it here.

Connections

In the Anker app, open your Solarbank, tap the gear icon, then Three-Party Control Settings. Turn on Modbus TCP and enter the IP address it shows.

Advanced

This address is set by SOLARBANK_HOST in the container settings. Remove that variable from your stack to change it here.

            Battery payback
            
        
      
            
      
            Enter what you paid for the battery and your electricity prices to see how long it takes to pay for itself.
            
      
            
      
            
      
            
        How this is worked out
        
            Each kWh the battery discharges is valued at the price for that half hour, as grid electricity it saved you buying. Charging costs the price for that half hour when the energy came from the grid, and your export rate when it came from solar you could have sold. Charging while the grid supplies power counts as grid charging. Totals start when this dashboard began recording. If you enter the install date, the energy the battery moved before then (from its own lifetime totals) is added, valued at the average price per kWh recorded so far. The projection needs at least a week of savings, and is shown as a range because savings per day vary a lot with the seasons.
