Battery payback

Enter what you paid for the battery and your electricity prices to see how long it takes to pay for itself.

How this is worked out

Each kWh the battery discharges is valued at the price for that half hour, as grid electricity it saved you buying. Charging costs the price for that half hour when the energy came from the grid, and your export rate when it came from solar you could have sold. Charging while the grid supplies power counts as grid charging. Totals start when this dashboard began recording, and the projection assumes future days save the same as the average so far.
